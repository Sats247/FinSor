View Methodology

Model Architecture

A Random Forest Regressor (120 trees, depth 8) is trained on 2,000 synthetic samples mapping [Age, Risk Score] → four ETF weight fractions. Separate models are trained per ETF and weights are L1-normalised to sum to 1.0.

Profile Thresholds

  • Aggressive (Risk ≥ 8): NIFTY 40%, BANK 30%, SILVER 25%, GOLD 5%
  • Moderate (Risk 5–7): NIFTY 50%, BANK 20%, GOLD 15%, SILVER 15%
  • Conservative (Risk < 5): GOLD 50%, NIFTY 35%, BANK 10%, SILVER 5%

Live Inference

Each slider adjustment triggers a debounced (320ms) POST to /api/predict-sip. The ML model runs inference server-side in <5ms. Allocations and rupee amounts are streamed back and animated into the UI without a page reload.
